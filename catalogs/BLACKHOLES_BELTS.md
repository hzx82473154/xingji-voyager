# Black holes and belt-member catalogs — current noncommercial edition

369 unique objects: 73 existing named research targets independently re-queried in SIMBAD, 291 unique systems from the MIT-licensed van den Bosch 2016 mass compilation (294 measurement rows), and five discovery-paper supplements. This bounded selection is not all known black holes. Former BlackCAT table values, raw strings and reference arrays are absent from current data; stable legacy IDs serve navigation only.

SIMBAD lookup components, queries, hashes, fields and transformations are in SIMBAD_BLACKHOLES.json and SIMBAD_COORDINATES.json; CDS, Strasbourg; ODbL1.0 https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France. The older 31-record coordinate component remains separately available, not a claim of current target parameters.

All369 have a source/host direction, not a resolved horizon. 305 have a literature distance; 227 have central mass estimates. For the73 replacement targets, use the latest available mesDistance literature measurement where present, never invert uncertain parallax or silently preserve old unlicensed table values. Unsupported mass/orbit values become null; classification is conservative and SIMBAD types alone do not establish a dynamical measurement. Null black-hole rotation is not a binary orbital period. Disk, jet and spin animations are illustrations.

The294-row mass compilation is MIT, Remco van den Bosch2016, ApJ831,134: https://github.com/remco-space/Black-Hole-Mass-compilation ; https://doi.org/10.3847/0004-637X/831/2/134 . Full license is SMBH_LICENSE.txt. Repeated NGC3227/4151/4395 measurements merge into291 systems with alternative measurements retained. Upper limits are candidates, not positive mass detections. Existing EHT2019 M87*, GaiaBH1–3, CygnusX-1 and TON618 discovery-paper facts and provenance remain. See original per-record source links; reference values are not copied article prose or illustrations.

146505 NASA/JPL SBDB belt records:139205 MBA rows with a catalogued diameter or rotation, and7300 TNO rows without a size cut. Scientific counts, arrays and orbital propagation remain unchanged. Compact columns, units, query/hash evidence and missing values are in belt-members-manifest.json and belt-members.json.gz. Two-body Kepler illustration is not a precision spacecraft ephemeris. TNO navigation includes wider trans-Neptunian populations, not only a strict classical Kuiper belt.

Refresh with `python scripts/import-blackholes-belts.py --belts-only` for JPL data. Use `python scripts/import-simbad-blackholes.py` for the independent current target component, reviewing changed classifications and preserving nulls. Original BlackCAT scraping is disabled; source-refresh must not recreate unconfirmed-permission tables. See NONCOMMERCIAL_SHARING.md and each database's independent license.
